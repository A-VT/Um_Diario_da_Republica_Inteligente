Um Diário da República Inteligente

Developed as part of a thesis project, this tool aims to help legal professionals and the general public to explore Portuguese legislation with ease. You need only ask a legal question, and the system uses standard Information Retrieval (IR) techniques and models to find the most relevant documents to your question from a consolidated legal corpus (sourced through Diário da República as of January 2025). Then, using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) approach, a large language model summarizes and answers your question based on the sources found. Click "Next" to begin.
